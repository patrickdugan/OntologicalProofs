# DE-03 · Rights, capacity and the limits of a public ledger

**Owner:** Codex CPU experimenter, separate blind ethical annotator. **Priority:** P0.

`MoralInformation.no_perfect_decoder` proves the generic collision obstruction from the assumed full obligation function. Extend `e05_relevant_invariance.py` from eight synthetic inputs to a preregistered generated case family where harm, consent, capacity, coercion, repair, and knowledge can vary independently. Give each factor an explicit moral relevance hypothesis and include a reviewer-ratified uncertainty marker.

## Matched comparisons

- Public-score-only classifier, same budget.
- Full-feature oracle (synthetic ceiling, never a model of divine knowledge).
- Causally constrained decision rule with explicit rights/appeal fields.
- Label-sensitive control and label-blinded counterfactual.

Hold out entire transformation families; assess false judgment, restitution omission, sensitivity to truly relevant changes, label-swap invariance when *declared relevant facts* are fixed, appeal corrections, and uncertainty escalations. Ensure the overall rate of correct authorized decisions is matched before comparing false approvals.

## Falsifiers

A seemingly invariant model may be invariant because it ignores the rights that need protection. A more detailed representation may expose sensitive private attributes, introducing a different injustice. Test both errors. Never collect real spiritual histories or classify living people by faith.
