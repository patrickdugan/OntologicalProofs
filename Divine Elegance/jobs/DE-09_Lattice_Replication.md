# DE-09 · A genuine lattice or an authored pattern?

**Owner:** independent mathematician and two blind chapter annotators. **Priority:** P2. **Hardware:** CPU.

Use the nineteen chapters and the author's E0–E8 keys. Create a blind coding rubric for concepts (unity, interpretation, mercy, agency, verification, rights, etc.), then annotate the chapters independently *without access to the author's lattice edges*. Compute formal concept closure, lattice order and Hasse covering relations from each rater's incidence matrix. Compare rater agreement and edit distances to the published lattice. Show exact assumptions and supply randomized theme/permuted-column baselines.

Falsifier: the lattice's striking cross-chapter patterns disappear under independent reasonable coding decisions. If so, preserve the original as a deliberately composed GEB device, not an independently discovered theorem about theology.

**Return:** coded incidence matrices, agreement report, lattice JSON, figure, baseline distribution, and proposed amendments to the author's explanation.
