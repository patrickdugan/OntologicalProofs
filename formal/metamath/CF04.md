# CF-04: well-founded root and arithmetic existential companions

Three certificates passed a full local replay against pinned set.mm with mmverify.py. This batch formalizes existence of a minimal element of a nonempty well-founded set, absence of a proper positive divisor of one, and the restricted existential statement of natural-number unboundedness. The source correspondence uses the classical set interpretation of Lean WellFounded; a machine-checked equivalence between Lean accessibility and set.mm foundedness is still open. Common-ancestor uniqueness and Reach construction remain outstanding. No philosophical or empirical conclusion is added. Scoped distinct-variable conditions are now included in the manifest audit. The independently checked CF-03 verifier is reused. CI for this extension is pending at registration.
