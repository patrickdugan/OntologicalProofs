## MR-01: first principle and objective value

Develop two separately stated routes to an attitude-independent reason relation.
One can use necessary perfection or actuality; the other can examine conditions
for agents to recognize reasons without arbitrary privileges. Fix what counts as
an evaluative fact before introducing divine attributes. Compare each route to
`ValueBridge` and identify which premise contributes genuinely normative content.

Target a theorem connecting the existing first principle to the justified reason
relation, with existence and consistency witnesses. A structure field equivalent
to the desired conclusion remains an open bridge. Test a unique ground with an
empty valuation, an inverted valuation, and several incompatible substantive
valuations. Investigate whether an Anderson-style modal setting avoids collapse
for the combined system; perform the modal proof on its actual semantics.

Acceptance requires independently motivated premises and a visible explanation
of their remaining contestability. Pure stipulation of goodness supplies no
completion credit for this work package.

## MR-02: justice and claims

Using E2-E5 as declared normative candidates, define harms, capacity and the
persistence of victim claims. Prove conditional invariance under changes of
morally irrelevant labels, while allowing decisions to change when capacity or
coercion changes. Separate forgiveness of an offender from satisfaction of a
victim's claim. Give an explicit rule for conflicts between claims.

Test uniformly adverse symmetric rules, label-blind disregard for harm, and
compression that merges cases with different required repairs. Produce a
nontrivial justice result whose normative source is inspectable. Use synthetic
cases only; classify no living person's faith or salvation.

## MR-03: guidance and defeaters

Extend the source-authentication interface with source identity, error models,
report selection and dependence among reports. Define a defeater as an explicit
change to the evidence model. Derive a rational-update result under those changed
likelihoods, with zero-event handling. Keep evidence for the truth of a claim and
evidence for the source's delegated authority in distinct fields.

Test a convincing unreliable report, duplicated reports from a common source,
selection on successful predictions, mistaken source identity and an empty
admissible-world set. A likelihood ratio chosen to guarantee the conclusion earns
no evidence credit. State which historical or empirical measurements would be
needed to apply the theorem to inspiration.

## MR-04: moral judgment, motivation and truthful refusal

Strengthen `maximizer_lawful` for bounded decision procedures. Investigate
lexicographic priority, constrained optimization and a reachable-policy invariant.
State existence or abstention conditions where lawful actions conflict. Include
truthful refusal of an illegitimate order in the action space.

Construct an informed violator, an agent whose moral penalty is outweighed by
reward, and a commitment-preserving agent tempted to conceal its behavior during
training. Seek conditions that favor truthful refusal while keeping useful
execution possible. Avoid defining rationality as obedience and then reporting
obedience as a discovery.

## MR-05: reachable execution and updates

Refine `ExecutionContract` into an executable specification for a harmless tool
sandbox. Bind proposals and receipts to subject, action payload, policy hash and
epoch. Model the atomic admission-and-execution step. Include every channel that
can produce an effect, as well as controller replacement and self-modification.

Prove the invariant by induction and provide a forward simulation from the toy
implementation into the specification. Test bypass, stale state, action rebind,
wrong-policy validation, forged receipt and an update that breaks preservation.
Preserve the finite audit's four premise-deletion witnesses. Demonstrate at least
one useful permitted task and report abstentions.

## MR-06: composition and training export

Compose reviewed MR-01 through MR-05 results without strengthening hidden
assumptions. Use one common model to witness joint consistency. Check that each
route's quantifiers and modal relations agree. A set of separate satisfying
models is insufficient evidence of their joint compatibility.

Export proof tasks as data carrying theorem ID, exact source revision, premises,
conclusion, open obligations and associated countermodels. Keep unknowns explicit.
Supply valid and invalid argument families for training, with splits by entire
proof-template family. Freeze the experiment contract in `EXPERIMENT.md` before
any GPU work. The source ledger must permit a reviewer to reject a normative
premise without corrupting the mathematical status of its conditional theorem.
