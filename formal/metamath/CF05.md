# CF-05: conditional unique first principle

Four additional set.mm certificates, 5,501 decoded proof steps:
- `ml-op-ancestor-of-root`: a root has no distinct ancestor under reflexive finite-path reachability.
- `ml-op-root-common-reach`: root plus a common ancestor yields reachability to the target.
- `ml-op-every-root-reaches`: under the global common-ancestor premise, every root reaches every element of the domain.
- `ml-op-unique-first-principle`: restricted unique existence, including the root and universal reachability conjuncts.

The domain A is a nonempty set, R is a typed predecessor relation contained in A x A, and R Fr A is set-theoretic foundedness. Reach is equality or nonempty transitive closure t++ R. The explicit nonemptiness witness B, foundedness, typed relation, and common-ancestor premise are retained. The proof uses the upstream foundedness, range of transitive closure, and restricted quantifier machinery. There are no new axioms. The theorem is conditional and does not assert that a physical or metaphysical domain satisfies its premises. Lean accessibility and inductive Reach are mapped to the set-theoretic notions by human correspondence; a verified translation between those foundations is not claimed.

The locally replayed addition is stored in standard compressed Metamath format, without saved-step opcodes Z; decoded reference counts are recorded in the manifest. The gate checks the proof encoding, counts, scope, distinct-variable declarations, source blobs, and runs both independent full-database verifiers. The negative control replaces the final proof body with an invalid explicit proof. The local Python full replay accepted the exact addition (Git blob d843e4687e2d61fb05084cb569746f4f96f65f77).
