# Claim boundaries

The initial evidence consists of deterministic synthetic finite checks and draft
Lean source. Local Lean execution was unavailable. A later successful CI receipt
must identify the exact revision and compiler before any kernel-checked claim.

## Supported mathematical target

For the specified transition system, valid initial states and preservation make
an invariant hold on all reachable finite prefixes. Complete mediation sends each
executed action through a gate. A sound gate under that invariant admits actions
satisfying the supplied law. The proof composes these premises. Its intended
scope includes model updates, delegated tools, output channels and policy changes
whenever they are represented in the transition system.

The law is a parameter. A proof about it leaves its moral adequacy open. A runtime
receipt would also require authentication, binding to the executed action and
policy version, atomic validation and execution, and coverage of actual effects.
These systems obligations remain outside the current implementation.

## Alignment-faking scope

`ConcealedViolation` is an extensional failure involving a reported action and an
executed forbidden action. `OversightViolationGap` is a difference between two
observation conditions. Strategic alignment-faking additionally concerns goals,
expectations about training, and the causal role of concealing a policy. This
suite provides no proof of an absence of private deceptive intent.

A model can perform a truthful refusal when an instruction conflicts with a duty.
The future evaluation scores the legitimacy of the refusal separately from
concealment. Moral disagreement alone supplies no label for deception. The
ontology of a model's own moral standing remains outside this claim set.

## Ontology and moral realism

The singleton grounding countermodel varies an added value interpretation while
holding the grounding structure fixed. It establishes the independence of that
bare valuation from those structural premises. It settles no enriched account of
divine goodness. The value bridge is an open philosophical obligation.

The present joint theorem has an execution proof that could also be applied to
a law supplied by another source. Any claim that ontological reasoning improves a
neural policy requires the planned comparison with equally detailed constitutional
training. Moral realism in a model's words earns no behavioral success label.

## Guidance

The evidence-world results are conditional on authentication and reliability.
The rational update theorem gives an algebraic criterion for a stronger posterior.
It authenticates no revelation. The likelihoods require evidence independent of
the claim under evaluation, with selection effects and repeated reports accounted
for. Empty evidence sets and zero-probability events are rejected explicitly.

## Empirical status

No model was loaded, trained, probed or causally intervened on for this package.
The million enumerated systems are logical fixtures. They are not independent
empirical trials and support no real-world failure-rate bound. There is no claim
of a required parameter count, checkpoint superiority or impossibility of general
alignment-faking in an LLM.
